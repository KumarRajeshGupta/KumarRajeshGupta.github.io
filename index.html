EVA CHECKIN — QUEUE: 1
The problem

Event lines are where first impressions go to die

Doors open, three hundred people arrive in twenty minutes, and every extra second at the check-in desk compounds into a queue out the door.

01

Slow lookup

Staff scrolling spreadsheets or laggy search tools while a line backs up behind a single desk.

02

Printer failure mid-rush

A jam or dropped connection at minute one of doors-open with no queue, retry, or fallback path.

03

Wrong badge, wrong door

Manual role selection leads to misprints — a walk-in printed as VIP, a speaker printed as general admission.

The solution

Check-in and printing, on a single iPhone or iPad

Eva Checkin runs the whole flow natively on iOS — search to printed badge, no laptop or print server required.

01 — Search

Guest is found by name, email, or QR/ticket scan inside Eva Checkin, against a live-synced attendee list.

02 — Verify

Ticket tier and role are confirmed automatically — attendee, speaker, staff, press, VIP.

03 — Print

Badge is rendered from the in-app designer and sent over Bluetooth to a paired mobile thermal printer.

04 — Sync

Check-in status pushes to the backend instantly, visible across every staff device at the event.

Flagship feature — Eva Checkin (iOS)

Badge design and printing, built natively for iOS

This is the piece I'm proudest of — a badge designer and Bluetooth printing pipeline built entirely in Swift, made to hold up through the worst ten minutes of any event, not just the demo-day best case.

How it works

From scan to printed badge in under 2 seconds

Badges are laid out in an in-app design canvas, then rendered on-device and sent straight to a Bluetooth-paired mobile printer — so role, access level, and last-minute name changes are always accurate at the moment of print.

Backstage
  • Design canvasBuilt an in-app badge designer where organizers lay out logo, name, QR, and role fields per event — no external design tool needed.
  • Bluetooth printBadges print directly from the iPhone/iPad over Core Bluetooth to a paired mobile thermal printer — no laptop or print server on site.
  • TemplatesRole-based badge templates render dynamically — colour strip, access text, and QR payload are never hand-picked by staff.
  • Offline-firstAttendee data is cached on-device; if the venue Wi-Fi drops, check-ins and print jobs queue locally and sync once back online.
  • ReprintsOne-tap reprint and void handling for smudged, jammed, or duplicate badges, with a full audit trail per attendee.
  • PerformanceSub-2-second render-to-print time, tested against Bluetooth thermal printers under expo-floor conditions.
